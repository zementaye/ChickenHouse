Cooked when you order it. Not before.

Doro means chicken in Amharic, and bet means house. Every piece is marinated overnight, then fried, grilled or simmered into wot when your order comes in. That is why the skin crackles and the meat stays juicy.

Come in for a family tray with injera, grab a wrap on your lunch break, or call ahead and collect.

Three ways to get it right

  1. Marinate

    Overnight in lemon, salt and berbere or awaze, so the flavour goes all the way to the bone.

  2. Cook

    Fried in small batches, grilled over charcoal, or simmered slowly into doro wot. You choose.

  3. Serve

    On the tray within minutes, with injera and sides made the same day.
